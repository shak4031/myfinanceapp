Bank connection or import issues

Include the institution name, approximate time, what you expected, and any error or request reference shown in the app. Redact personal data from screenshots.

MyFinance cannot move money or fix a bank's account balance. Contact your bank directly for disputed charges, suspected fraud or urgent payment issues.

Keep sensitive information out of messages

Never email passwords, verification codes, full account numbers, access tokens or unredacted statements. Support will not ask you to email your bank password. General questions do not require access to your financial workspace.

MyFinance does not move money. Planning results are estimates.
