Import a statement

Bring your transactions into one clear view.

Import one file at a time. Wait for its result before starting another CSV, OFX or PDF import. Multiple-file drops are not supported.

Used to match transfers, so moving money isn't counted as spending.

Use a CSV, OFX or QFX bank export. Dates, columns and amounts are detected automatically. Accounts are matched from the file, including multi-account CSVs. New or unidentified accounts are reviewed before anything is imported.

Account detected from your statement

Upload an original bank or credit card PDF. Balances and transactions are checked before saving.

Text-based, unlocked PDFs only. For scans or protected files, use a CSV/OFX export instead. Purchase APR is captured when readable.

Import history & recovery

Selecting a file never changes your data. Select imports, review the validated impact, acknowledge it, then confirm. Undo is reversible using its retained Restore backup.

Undo removes only transactions added by the selected imports and keeps a server-side backup for Restore. Skipped duplicates are never removed. Accounts and saved rules stay in place.

Older imports are inferred from filename, account and saved timestamp. Verify them carefully: previous PDF statement balances cannot be recovered for these older imports.
