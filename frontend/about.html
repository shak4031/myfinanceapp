ABOUT MYFINANCE

Clarity for your money. Care for your data.

MyFinance is an independently built household finance app. Its purpose is simple: help you understand spending, keep track of bills, and make thoughtful plans for savings and debt.

Built for everyday decisions

Your finances should be understandable without a spreadsheet marathon. MyFinance brings transactions, categories and planning into one workspace, while keeping recorded facts separate from forecasts. You choose your next step; the app does not move your money.

Trust should come from safeguards, not company size

It is reasonable to be careful with financial data. Rather than asking you to rely on a big-company name or vague promises, we explain the protections built into MyFinance.

Read the technical security whitepaper for data-flow diagrams, cryptographic parameters, TLS responsibilities, account isolation and the limits of these safeguards.

Separate financial workspaces

Database-enforced access rules separate households. Sharing happens within your household; another customer's login does not grant access to your workspace.

Protected sign-in and sessions

Passwords are stored as salted, computationally hardened hashes, not readable passwords. Production connections use HTTPS, and sign-in sessions expire after 10 minutes without interaction.

Optional, data-only bank connections

Connect an eligible U.S. bank through Plaid or import statements yourself. MyFinance stores the Plaid sync token encrypted with AES-256-GCM. This integration retrieves account data; it cannot initiate payments or transfers.

You control support access

Customer-workspace support access requires your approval, is time-limited and audited, and can be revoked. Read-only support is enforced by the database, not just a screen setting.

Clear limits, no inflated promises

No online service can guarantee zero risk. These safeguards are not a claim of independent security certification, a guarantee against breaches, or a promise that server operators cannot access stored financial records. Authorized operators retain infrastructure access.

You can start without linking a bank and disconnect any time to stop future bank updates. Previously imported transactions remain in your history. Questions about privacy or security deserve a direct answer: contact MyFinance.

Useful guidance, honest expectations

Planning tools are educational, not financial, tax or legal advice. Forecasts, payment estimates and payoff dates depend on the information available and are not promises.
